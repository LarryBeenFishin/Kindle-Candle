Why this setup works

Materials hold the prices

Update a wax, jar, wick, or fragrance cost once. Every preset that uses it immediately reflects the new cost.

Presets hold the recipe

A preset remembers only what goes into one candle, keeping recipes separate from changing purchase prices and batch sizes.

The calculator stays simple

Select a preset, enter how many candles you are making, and get the current cost, batch requirements, margin, and projected profit.
