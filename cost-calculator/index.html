How the invoice import works

1. Read

For normal PDFs, the tool first looks for selectable text. For images and scanned documents, it can use browser-based OCR to read the page.

2. Review

Likely material lines, quantities, prices, shipping, and tax are placed into editable fields. You stay in control of every number used.

3. Calculate

Choose how much of each material you use in one candle, then see landed cost, batch cost, profit, margin, and pricing targets.

Invoice privacy: this prototype processes invoice content inside your browser. It does not intentionally send the invoice to Kindle Candle or store the uploaded file. OCR and PDF helper libraries are loaded from third-party CDNs. For sensitive business records, always review the privacy requirements of any production implementation.
