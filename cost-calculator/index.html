Saved Candles

Reuse a recipe whenever material prices change. Because recipes point to inventory items, updated material costs automatically flow into the calculation when you load the candle again.

Why this setup works

Buy once, use everywhere

Store a wax, jar, wick, or fragrance cost once. Every candle recipe can reuse that material without typing the purchase price again.

Invoices feed inventory

An invoice can contain supplies for several different products. Importing into inventory first keeps those purchases separate from any single candle recipe.

Reorders stay simple

When an imported material matches something you already have, add the new quantity and update its current unit cost instead of creating duplicate costing entries.
