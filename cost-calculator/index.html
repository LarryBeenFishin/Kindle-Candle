A simpler way to cost candles

Start with purchase cost

Enter the actual price you paid for a case, bottle, spool, or pack. The calculator converts that purchase into the cost of the amount used in one candle.

Include your time

Materials are only part of the cost. Add the time spent producing a batch and the hourly value of that labor so your pricing reflects the work involved.

Price from margin

Instead of blindly multiplying your cost, choose the gross margin you want. The suggested price updates automatically as your materials or costs change.
